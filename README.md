## The problem
When a person enters a hospital, with signs of a stroke, the doctor must know whether this person has brain bleeding or a blocked blood vessel. Without the proper diagnosis and identification of the problem, things might get messy. In worse cases, there might not even be a radiologist on site. Therefore, we have created a prototype model to detect brain bleeding, ischemia (blocked or narrowed arteries), or a normal situation through providing it CT scans of the brain. 
## What we built
At the start, we created a linear regression model but it wasn't too great (accuracy was 7%) so we used a prebuilt model. We are using a prebuilt model by the name of ResNet18 to classify harmful brain diseases. The accuracy of the ResNet18 was initially 82%, after training and validating this model, the accuracy increased to 92%. Our model is simply an image classification model. We will be classifying whether a person is completely normal, has a condition of Ischemia, or brain bleeding. The images given to the model will be of a brain CT scan which shows whether it has a tumor or is a normal.
